Privacy Policy & Privacy Notice

Document Ref: RM-PRIVACY-2026-V1 | Effective 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how realmate ("we," "us," or "the Platform") collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects personal information. We are committed to processing personal data in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). Our processing is guided by the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality.

INDEPENDENCE NOTICE: realmate is an independent platform and is not affiliated with, owned by, operated by, sponsored by, or endorsed by Alveo Land Corp., Ayala Land, Inc., or their affiliates. Personal information processed through realmate is handled by the realmate development group, not by any third-party real estate company.

SECTION 1: INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1. Account & Profile Data: Name, professional role, business email address (e.g., an @alveoland.com address used for verification), profile photo, contact details, and credentials you provide for verification.

1.2. Listing & Marketplace Data: Property listings, requirements, locations, pricing, specifications, images, and other content you post or share on the Platform.

1.3. Communications: Messages, chats, connection requests, and other communications you exchange with other users through the Platform.

1.4. Technical Data: Device information, log data, and usage information generated when you access the Platform, used for security and to keep the service running.

SECTION 2: HOW WE USE INFORMATION (LEGITIMATE PURPOSE)

We process personal information only for purposes that are declared, specified, and legitimate, including to:

  • Create and manage your account and verify your eligibility to access the Platform.
  • Operate the AI matching engine that surfaces relevant buyers, sellers, and partners.
  • Enable connections, messaging, and collaboration between verified users.
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the Platform.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not collect or process more information than is necessary to achieve these purposes (proportionality).

SECTION 3: SHARING & DISCLOSURE

3.1. Between Users: Your profile and the listings or requirements you post are visible to, and may be matched with, other verified users. This peer-to-peer visibility is the core function of the Platform.

3.2. Consent of Third Parties: If you input personal information belonging to your clients (buyers or sellers), you represent that you have obtained their consent as required by the Data Privacy Act before doing so.

3.3. No Sale of Data: We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law, court order, or a lawful request from a competent authority.

SECTION 4: DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

We implement reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorized access, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described here or as required by law.

SECTION 5: YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA SUBJECT

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to object, to access, to rectify, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, to damages, and to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

SECTION 6: CONTACT

For questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact the realmate development group at reychanbernaldez1019@gmail.com. You also have the right to contact the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) regarding the processing of your personal data.

SECTION 7: UPDATES
