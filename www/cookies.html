Cookie & Tracking Notice

Document Ref: RM-COOKIES-2026-V1  |  Effective Date: September 26, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 26, 2026

This Notice explains how realmate uses cookies, local storage, session storage, and similar technologies (together, "storage technologies") on the web and mobile Platform. It forms part of, and should be read together with, our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

SUMMARY: realmate uses only essential storage technologies needed to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and make core features work. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, and we do not sell your data for advertising.

SECTION 1: WHAT THESE TECHNOLOGIES ARE

Storage technologies are small pieces of data placed on your device or held by your browser or app. They let the Platform recognize your session, keep settings, and operate reliably. On our mobile app, similar data may be held in the app's local storage and, where you enable it, in secure device storage for keeping you signed in.

SECTION 2: WHAT WE USE AND WHY

CategoryPurposeEssential?
Authentication & sessionKeep you securely signed in and maintain your session across pages and app screens.Yes
PreferencesRemember choices such as onboarding state, filters, and interface settings.Yes
Security & integrityHelp detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.Yes
FunctionalityEnable core features such as notifications, matching, and messaging.Yes

We do not use storage technologies for third-party advertising or cross-site advertising profiles.

SECTION 3: ANALYTICS

Where we measure how the Platform is used to keep it working and improve it, we do so in a limited, privacy-respecting way and do not use this information for third-party advertising. Any analytics processing is described in our Privacy Policy.

SECTION 4: MANAGING STORAGE TECHNOLOGIES

Because the storage we use is essential to how the Platform operates, there is no separate advertising opt-out to configure. You can still:

  • Clear your browser's cookies and local storage, or your app data, through your browser or device settings; and
  • Sign out to end your session.

Clearing essential storage may sign you out and reset preferences, and some features may not work correctly until the storage is re-created.

SECTION 5: CHANGES

We may update this Notice from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

SECTION 6: CONTACT

For questions about this Notice, contact realmate at support@realmate.ph.
