Mission & Engineering Vision

realmate was pioneered by Reychan Bernaldez (Product & Growth) alongside an elite multi-disciplinary founding team: Eris Dungug (Sales & Technology), Danniel Libor (Software Engineer), Jaztin Pumaren (QA & Testing), and Mark Hequibal (Marketing).

By integrating Advanced Artificial Intelligence, we have engineered a solution built for real estate professionals — currently exclusive to the Alveo Sales Team. We recognize that the modern real estate landscape demands more than manual effort; it requires algorithmic precision to handle complex reselling and portfolio management.

Our platform utilizes AI to analyze market data in real-time, helping professionals find the absolute closest match for their clients' property resale needs. We bridge the gap between financial strategy, technical engineering, and marketing expertise to ensure every transaction is optimized for success.
