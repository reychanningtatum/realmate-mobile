What is realmate

The AI matching network built for
real estate professionals.

realmate is an intelligent platform where real estate professionals post what they have and what they need — then let AI instantly surface the right buyers, sellers, and partners to transact with. It turns scattered listings and group-chat guesswork into precise, real-time matches.

The problem

Deals get lost in the noise.

  • Buyer and seller requirements are buried across group chats, spreadsheets, and scattered posts.
  • Finding the right counterpart for a client takes hours of manual searching and asking around.
  • Perfect-fit matches are missed simply because the right two agents never crossed paths in time.
  • No single, trusted place for the sales team to collaborate and move inventory.
The solution

realmate matches them for you.

  • Post a listing or a requirement once — AI does the searching across the entire network.
  • Get ranked, percentage-scored matches by location, unit type, and price in seconds.
  • Connect and negotiate instantly with the exact agent who has what your client needs.
  • One professional home for real estate professionals to transact and grow together.
How it works

From listing to closed deal in four steps.

A workflow designed to remove the busywork so you can focus on your clients.

STEP 01

Post your listing

Add what you're selling — or what your buyer is looking for. Location, unit type, and price is all it takes.

STEP 02

AI finds matches

The engine analyzes thousands of data points and surfaces the closest counterparts, ranked by match score.

STEP 03

Connect & negotiate

Chat in real time, add the agent as a realmate, and send or receive offers — all inside the app.

STEP 04

Close the deal

Move from match to transaction faster, with the right partner and the data to back every decision.
